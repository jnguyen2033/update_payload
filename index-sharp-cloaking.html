Kinematics

Kinematics is the study of motion without considering its causes.

Learning focus

Identify the quantities in the central relationship. Explain what each symbol means and state your units.

01 / Central relationship

v = Δx / Δt

Read the relationship in words before substituting numbers. List the known quantities, check the units, and explain whether your answer makes sense.

02 / Worked example

A cart travels 24 m in 6 s. Find its average velocity.

Show solution

24 ÷ 6 = 4 m/s.
